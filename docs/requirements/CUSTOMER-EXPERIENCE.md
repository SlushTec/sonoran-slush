# Customer experience contract

## Navigation and presentation

Shop Gifts / Custom & Bulk / Live Events / Our Work / Help. Include all documented product families;
crystals are prominent but not the whole brand. Product/occasion/budget discovery and resilient search
must work without chat. Real photos, legible type, navy/gold identity, consistent responsive components.
Never invent testimonials, scarcity, discounts, product photography or a real manufacturing preview.

## Customizer

Show only relevant options; distinguish text, logos, photos, placements, color fill and shared/mixed names.
Use the customer's actual text for font preview. Preserve input through back/refresh/payment failure.
Anonymous saved work is supported with an explicit privacy/expiry policy; private artwork never enters analytics.
Upload previews/progress/retries, clear formats/limits, HEIC handling after validation, accessible crop controls.
A placement mockup is not a production proof or physically accurate subsurface simulation.
Show exact configuration, included/paid options and server-confirmed total before commitment.

## Checkout, gifts and delivery

Guest checkout is prominent; account creation optional after purchase. Autofill, mobile keyboards and field errors.
Express payment cannot skip required personalization. No hidden mandatory fees or preselected paid add-ons.
Separate artwork deadline, production estimate and estimated arrival; disclose uncertainty and review deadlines.
Gift message, actual packaging, recipient delivery and price-free receipt options where approved.
Do not send surprise-spoiling messages or marketing to recipients by default.

## Quotes and events

Separate shared-design bulk pricing from unique names. Capture requirements before demanding final artwork.
Quote portal shows status, next step, submitted files and current accepted version.
An event inquiry is not a confirmed booking. Clearly distinguish requested/held/confirmed dates,
travel, quantities, machine/operator capacity, deposits, balances and preparation deadlines.

## After purchase

Meaningful order timeline, authorized guest access, version-specific proof review with approve/change,
shipment/pickup info, issue reporting, care and safe reorder with current price/availability checks.

## Assistance and failure behavior

Catalog-grounded cards and editable constraints, not a chat wall. Explicit customer consent before any action.
AI cannot promise price, stock, production dates, approval or engraving suitability absent authoritative checks.
Checkout/catalog remain usable on model failure. No blocking chat popups, forced registration or artificial urgency.
Every async action has bounded waiting, a real status and recoverable next steps; no lost personalization.

## Measurement

Separate retail purchase, bulk quote-acceptance and event-confirmation funnels. Measure remakes/refunds/deadlines too.
Analytics must exclude artwork, names/inscriptions, contact details, private chat and payment data.
Current scaffold uses no customer analytics. Consent and event schema require review before enabling telemetry.
