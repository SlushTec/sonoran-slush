# 3D LaserBox Jet Mini — Capabilities

**Equipment behind Sonoran Slush’s portable / on-site 3D crystal service.**

The Jet Mini series (by 3D LaserBox / 3D Crystal) is a compact, air-cooled, green-beam subsurface laser system designed specifically for professional 3D crystal engraving that can travel to events.

---

## Why It Matters for Sonoran Slush

- First-in-Arizona portable professional-quality 3D crystal engraver claim rests on this class of machine.
- Enables live production at weddings, graduations, reunions, corporate events, markets, and other social gatherings across AZ / NM / Southern California.
- Guests can watch the piece being made and leave with a finished keepsake the same day.

## Model Overview

| Feature | JetMini Express | JetMini Original |
|---------|-----------------|------------------|
| **Max crystal size** | ~3.5" × 2.5" (10 × 6 cm) | ~6" × 4" (15 × 10 cm) |
| **Field of view (height)** | ~8 cm | ~12 cm |
| **Machine weight** | ~36 lb / 16.5 kg | ~49 lb / 23 kg |
| **Approx. dimensions** | 7.5 × 11 × 13.75 in | 10.25 × 13.25 × 18.5 in |
| **Best for** | Events, fast small items, keychains, high volume of smaller pieces | Larger crystals, awards, broader product mix, ecommerce + events |
| **Portability** | Event-optimized, fits travel case | Still portable, larger capacity |

Both models share the same core laser performance (~100,000 points/min class) and green-beam subsurface quality.

---

## Core Technical Capabilities

- **Laser type:** Green-beam (532 nm class) diode-pumped subsurface engraving
- **Process:** True internal 3D point-cloud engraving inside optical / K9 crystal (not surface etching)
- **Burn speed:** Approximately 100,000 points per minute (typical medium block in roughly 5–8 minutes; keychains often 1–2 minutes)
- **Cooling:** Air-cooled — no water chiller required
- **Power:** Runs on standard household outlets (~500 W class, 110–220 V)
- **Noise:** Low (office / event friendly)
- **Workflow support:** Designed to work with AI / Cockpit3D-style 2D→3D conversion so a customer photo becomes a producible point cloud quickly
- **Visual engagement:** Models with transparent / filtered doors let guests watch the engraving happen live

---

## Practical On-Site Strengths

1. **Portable** — Packs into a travel case; single-person movable; sets up on a standard table in minutes.
2. **Fast enough for events** — Keychains and smaller pieces in minutes; medium crystals in a single-digit number of minutes.
3. **Professional quality** — Subsurface 3D (floating image inside the crystal), not a surface mark or UV novelty.
4. **Crowd-friendly** — Guests can see the process; transparent door variants turn production into a show.
5. **Standard power** — No special venue electrical requirements in most cases.

---

## Typical Workflow (Event or Studio)

1. Customer provides or we capture a photo.
2. Photo is digitized, enhanced, and converted to a 3D point cloud (AI-assisted / Cockpit3D-class tools).
3. Customer previews the result in the chosen crystal shape/size.
4. On approval, the Jet Mini engraves the piece.
5. Finished crystal is handed over (or shipped within the stated turnaround for remote orders).
