## Custom 3D Crystals

**How does a 3D crystal work?**  
We take your photo, convert it into a detailed 3D point cloud, and use a professional green-beam laser to engrave millions of tiny points *inside* a solid piece of optical / K9 crystal. The image appears to float inside the crystal. It is not printed or etched on the surface.

**What machine do you use?**  
We use a **3D LaserBox Jet Mini Original** — a portable, air-cooled, professional subsurface laser. It handles crystals up to approximately 6" × 4" (15 × 10 cm) and is designed for both studio and live event work.

**Can I see a preview before it is engraved?**  
Yes. After we process your photo we send a preview link showing the image inside the crystal shape you chose. Production begins only after you approve it.

**How long does it take?**  
- **On-site / events:** Many pieces are completed in minutes while you wait.  
- **Remote / shipped orders:** Once the preview is approved, most orders are completed and shipped within 24 hours (major holidays excepted).

**What kind of photo works best?**  
Clear, well-lit photos with good contrast. We can work with almost any image and will digitize, enhance, and optimize it for free. High-resolution is ideal but not required.

**Can you remove backgrounds or do multiple people?**  
Yes. Background removal and multi-subject optimization are part of the standard preparation process.

**Are the wood products food-safe?**

## Care & Quality

**How do I care for a 3D crystal?**  
Dust with a soft cloth. Avoid harsh cleaners and extreme temperature changes. The internal engraving will not fade.

**What if I’m not happy with the result?**  
We work with you through the preview stage so the final piece matches expectations. Contact us immediately if there is any issue with a delivered order.
