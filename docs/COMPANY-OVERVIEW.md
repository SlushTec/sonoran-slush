# Sonoran Slush Creative Creations — Company Overview

## Identity

- **Legal / Operating Name:** Sonoran Slush Creative Creations
- **Parent / Umbrella:** SlushTec
- **Location:** Tucson, Arizona
- **Domain:** sonoranslush.net
- **Type:** Fully custom-order laser engraving and personalized gift business

## Core Offering

Primary product line centers on **3D subsurface laser-engraved crystal gifts** created from customer-supplied photographs. Supporting services include other laser engraving work (wood, acrylic, metal, etc.) as equipment and capacity allow.

Typical use cases:
- Graduation gifts
- Memorial / remembrance pieces
- Anniversaries and family keepsakes
- Local market and event sales

## Ordering Model

Fully custom. Customers provide a photo and select product options (size, shape, bases, lighting, inscriptions, keychains, etc.). Production is made-to-order.

Target customer experience mirrors high-quality photo-to-crystal configurators:
1. Choose product / size
2. Select add-ons
3. Upload photo
4. Provide details
5. Checkout

## Strategic Role Inside SlushTec

1. **Near-term revenue and local brand presence** via custom gifts and market appearances.
2. **Future official merchandise channel** for SlushTec intellectual property (VARP, Ashcourt — Hell Rises, studio apparel, residual-themed items, limited runs, etc.).
3. Keeps manufacturing / fulfillment capability in-house under the same ownership structure.

## Constraints & Philosophy

- Home / small-shop based operation
- Emphasis on quality and personalization over high-volume commodity product
- No external investors with directional control (aligned with overall SlushTec posture)
