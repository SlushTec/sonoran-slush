# Product Page Scaffolding — Jet Mini Original Crystals

**Purpose:** Distinct, captivating page structure and copy direction for each producible shape.  
Use as the blueprint for Shopify product pages and the website catalog.

**Shared rules for every page**
- Only list sizes from the official keep list
- Always offer preview before engraving
- Recommend light base where it elevates the piece
- Photo upload + free digitize/enhance/optimize
- 24-hour ship after approval (major holidays excepted) or same-day at events
