# Photo Conversion Guide — What Works in 3D Crystal

**Last updated:** 2026-08-11  
**Purpose:** Help customers choose photos that convert beautifully into subsurface 3D crystal images.

---

## How the Laser Sees Your Photo

The Jet Mini Original burns a 3D image by placing tiny white dots *inside* the crystal.

- **Bright / light areas** in the photo → more laser dots → denser, brighter, more visible details in the crystal
- **Black / dark areas** in the photo → fewer (or no) laser dots → clearer / more transparent areas in the crystal

This is the opposite of a regular print. In crystal, darkness becomes clarity and light becomes the visible image.

---

## Photos That Convert Well

| Type | Why it works |
|------|--------------|
| **Good contrast** | Clear separation between subject and background gives strong depth |
| **Well-lit faces** | Eyes, smile lines, and features stay readable |
| **Single or few subjects** | More dots can be dedicated to each person or pet |
| **Medium to close framing** | Faces and important details fill the crystal |
| **Clean or simple backgrounds** | Subject stands out; fewer competing dots |
| **Pets with clear features** | Fur texture and eyes read nicely when contrast is good |
| **High-resolution or sharp phone photos** | More detail survives the 2D→3D conversion |

**Best everyday examples**
- Outdoor portraits with soft natural light
- Studio-style photos with even lighting
- Pet close-ups against a simple backdrop
- Couple or family shots where faces are clearly visible

---

## Photos That Convert Poorly

| Type | Problem |
|------|---------|
| **Very dark / underexposed images** | Almost no dots → weak or nearly invisible image |
| **Low contrast / foggy / hazy** | Details blend together; depth collapses |
| **Busy, cluttered backgrounds** | Background competes with the subject for dots |
| **Heavy shadows on faces** | Eyes or one side of the face can disappear |
| **Group shots with many tiny faces** | Each face gets too few dots to stay clear |
| **Blurry or motion-blurred photos** | Soft edges become mushy in crystal |
| **Text-heavy or logo-only graphics** | May work, but pure photo portraits usually look better |
| **Extreme filters / heavy vignettes** | Can confuse the conversion and reduce detail |

---

## Quick Rules of Thumb

1. **Light on the subject is your friend.** The brighter and clearer the important parts of the photo, the stronger the crystal image.
2. **Dark backgrounds are often excellent.** They become clear crystal and make the subject pop.
3. **We can help.** Background removal, contrast adjustment, and optimization are part of our free preparation process.
4. **When in doubt, send it.** We will tell you honestly if a photo needs a different crop or a better source image.

## Technical Note (internal)

Because the laser places dots where the image is bright, a mostly black source file produces a mostly clear crystal with only the light details visible. This is why underexposed or heavily shadowed photos often look weak, and why we routinely adjust levels and remove backgrounds before conversion.
