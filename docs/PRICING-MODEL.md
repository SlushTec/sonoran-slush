## 1. Cost stack (what every order must cover)

| Cost | Typical range | Notes |
|------|---------------|--------|
| **Blank cost** | Varies by product | Your landed cost after shipping from supplier |
| **Engraving time / wear** | $1–$4 | Laser time + consumables |
| **Packaging** | $1–$3 | Box, tissue, label |
| **Shopify Payments** | ~2.9% + $0.30 | Basic plan (lower on higher plans) |
| **Shopify platform** | amortized | Monthly plan cost spread across orders |
| **Outbound shipping** | $6–$18 | USPS Ground Advantage / Priority; boards & sets higher |
| **Returns / defects buffer** | 2–3% | Small pad |
| **Target gross margin** | 40–50%+ after fees | After all above |

**Arizona tax note**  
You collect TPT (state 5.6% + local) on taxable sales once you have nexus. Tax is *added at checkout* — it is not taken from your margin. Price your products pre-tax; Shopify Tax or manual rates handle the rest. Tucson combined is typically ~8.7%.

**Shipping is the big variable.** Free shipping must be baked into the product price or gated behind a cart threshold.

## 2. Free-shipping target

**Recommended free-shipping threshold: $75**

| Why $75 |
|--------|
| Covers a single mid-tier board, tumbler, or decanter set with room |
| Two smaller items (keychain + journal, rimmer + opener, etc.) clear it easily |
| High enough that pure low-ticket favor orders still pay shipping |
| Competitive with common Etsy / small-shop thresholds ($50–$99) |

**How to implement**
- Orders **under $75** → customer pays calculated shipping (or a flat $7.95 / $9.95)
- Orders **$75+** → free USPS Ground Advantage / Priority
- Optional: always free shipping on **3D subsurface crystal** (Jet Mini) photo products if margin allows

Average shipping you will actually pay (commercial rates, 2026):
- Small item (keychain, opener, ornament, journal, wine stopper): **$5–$8**
- Tumbler / water bottle / rimmer: **$8–$12**
- Single cutting board: **$10–$15**
- Board set or whiskey set: **$14–$20**

Bake ~$9–11 average shipping cost into every free-ship order.

## 5. Simple margin check formula

```
Retail
− Shopify fee (~2.9% + $0.30)
− Blank cost
− Packaging
− Allocated shipping (or actual if under threshold)
− Engraving cost
= Contribution margin
```

**Target:** ≥ 40% contribution after the above on most items; 50%+ on high-volume small goods and 3D crystal.

Example (Acacia Medium at $42):
- Shopify ~$1.52
- Blank (est.) $8–12
- Ship allocation $10
- Pack + laser $3
- **Rough left: $15–20** before your time → healthy if blank cost stays controlled.

## 6. Rules of thumb

1. **Never** price below the sum of blank + ship + fees + $5 minimum labor.
2. **Sets** should look like a deal vs buying three singles, but still clear margin.
3. **Volume / favor items** (keychains, openers) can run tighter margin if they pull cart value toward the $75 free-ship threshold.
4. **3D crystal photo products** are the brand differentiator — protect higher margins there.
5. **Surface ornaments + wine stopper** are volume/gift add-ons — keep priced accessibly.
6. **Service 10%** (vet / first responder / teacher) is standing policy — see DISCOUNT-POLICY.md.
7. Revisit prices after you know exact landed blank costs and real average shipping zones.
