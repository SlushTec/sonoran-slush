# Crystal Selection Guide

**For Sonoran Slush Creative Creations — Jet Mini Original**

This guide helps customers (and the team) choose the right crystal shape, size, and options for a given photo and occasion. All sizes listed fit within the Jet Mini Original’s working volume (max ≈ 6″ × 4″ / 15 × 10 cm).

---

## How to Choose

1. **Start with the photo** — portrait, landscape, group, pet, or single subject?
2. **Decide the use** — desk display, keychain, award, guest favor, memorial, or gift?
3. **Pick size** — smaller = faster & more portable; larger = more presence and detail.
4. **Add a base or light** if the piece will be displayed (strongly recommended for most desk/gift crystals).
5. **Optional inscription** — short text, date, or names.

---

## Recommended Crystal Styles

### Rectangle (Tall / Portrait)
- Best for: single person, couple portraits, graduation photos, formal headshots
- Orientation: vertical
- Why it works: natural fit for upright subjects; strong desk presence

### Rectangle (Wide / Landscape)
- Best for: group photos, family shots, scenic moments, pets side-by-side
- Orientation: horizontal
- Why it works: captures more of the scene without cropping tightly

### Heart
- Best for: couples, anniversaries, weddings, romantic gifts, memorials
- Emotional weight: high
- Note: shape itself carries meaning — pair with a clean, well-lit photo

### Cube / Diamond / Prestige shapes
- Best for: awards, corporate gifts, milestone recognition, premium keepsakes
- Presence: high
- Works well with bases and lighting

### Keychain (Rectangle or Heart)
- Best for: guest favors, on-site event giveaways, everyday carry keepsakes
- Speed: fastest burn times (often 1–2 minutes)
- Ideal for live event production where volume and handoff speed matter

## Photo Tips for Best Results

- **Clear subject** — face or main subject should be sharp and well-lit.
- **Good contrast** — avoids washed-out or overly dark results inside the crystal.
- **Simple background** when possible — busy backgrounds can compete with the 3D effect.
- **Avoid heavy filters** — natural photos convert more cleanly.
- **Groups** — closer crops usually work better than very wide distant shots.
- We digitize, enhance, and optimize every photo at no extra charge before engraving.

| Add-on | When to recommend |

## Occasion Quick Picks

- **Wedding / Anniversary** → Heart or tall rectangle + light base + inscription
- **Graduation** → Tall rectangle or prestige shape + “Class of …” inscription
- **Memorial / Celebration of Life** → Clean rectangle or heart, respectful base, optional inscription
- **Corporate / Award** → Larger rectangle, cube, or prestige + wooden base
- **Guest favor / Event** → Keychain (rectangle or heart)
- **Pet** → Medium rectangle or heart, clear subject photo
- **Family / Group** → Wide rectangle
