# Crystal Size Keep List — Jet Mini Original

**Source:** Official size charts + Year of the JetMini 2025 PDF (p.7)  
**Hard limit:** Up to **6" × 4" (15 × 10 cm)**  
**Rule:** Only shapes/sizes the Jet Mini Original can produce appear in customer-facing marketing and the Crystal Selection Guide.

---

## KEEP (Producible)

### 3D Dog Bone
- One size: **6" × 4" (15 × 10 cm)** — Vertical & Horizontal orientations  
  ✅ Exactly at max capacity — KEEP

### Candle
- One size: **4" × 2.4 × 2.4 (10 × 6 × 6 cm)**  
  ✅ KEEP

### Cut Corner Diamond
- Small: 2" × 2 × 2 (5 × 5 × 5 cm)  
- Medium: 2.4" × 2.4 × 2.4 (6 × 6 × 6 cm)  
- Large: 3" × 3 × 3 (8 × 8 × 8 cm)  
  ✅ All KEEP

### Heart
- Small: 3.2" × 2.8 × 1.6 (8 × 7 × 4 cm)  
- Medium: 4" × 3.5 × 2.4 (10 × 9 × 5 cm)  
- Large: 5" × 4.3 × 2.4 (13 × 11 × 5 cm)  
  ✅ All KEEP

### Prestige Iceberg
- Small only: **5" × 4.3 × 2.4 (13 × 11 × 6 cm)**  
  ✅ KEEP  
- Medium (6.8") & Large (8") → QUARANTINE

### Rectangle Tall
- Small: 2.5" × 1.5 × 1.5  
- Medium: 3" × 2 × 2  
- Large: 3.5" × 2.5 × 2.5  
- XLarge: 4.8" × 3.2 × 2.4  
- **Mini Mantel: 6" × 4 × 2.5 (15 × 10 × 6 cm)** → KEEP (at limit)  
- Large Mantel / Mini Presidential / Large Presidential → QUARANTINE

### Rectangle Wide
- Same as Tall: Small through **Mini Mantel (6" × 4")** → KEEP  
- Larger sizes → QUARANTINE

### Keychains & Jewelry (all KEEP)
- Heart Keychain: 3.5 × 3.5 × 1.2 cm  
- Rectangle Keychain: 3 × 2 × 1.5 cm  
- 2D Crystal Heart Necklace: 3.5 × 3.5 × 0.8 cm  
- 2D Crystal Rectangle Necklace: 1.2" × 1.6 (3 × 4 cm)

---

## QUARANTINE (Do not show as available)

- Prestige Iceberg Medium & Large  
- Rectangle Tall/Wide: Large Mantel, Mini Presidential, Large Presidential  
- Any crystal candle/urn or globe styles not confirmed within 6×4  
- Any marketing that implies sizes beyond 6" × 4"

---

## Notes from JetMini 2025 PDF

- Official claim: *“Can burn more than 80% of the shapes/sizes up to 6 x 4\" (15 x 10 cm)”*
- Designed for events, tabletop shows, portability
- Key value: burn in minutes + big viewing window so customers watch

---

## Website / Selection Guide Rule

Only list the KEEP sizes above.  
Mini Mantel (6×4) is the practical maximum for both Tall and Wide rectangles and the Dog Bone.
