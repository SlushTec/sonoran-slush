# Business source register

## Preserved, not re-priced

All product files in `docs/products/` are retained byte-for-byte, together with COMPANY-OVERVIEW,
CRYSTAL-SELECTION-GUIDE, CRYSTAL-SIZE-KEEP-LIST, DISCOUNT-POLICY, EVENTS, FAQ, FUTURE-MERCH,
GLASSWARE-COLOR-FILL, GLASSWARE-PRICING, JET-MINI-CAPABILITIES, MARKETING-ASSETS, ON-SITE-EVENTS,
OWNERS_PERSONALITY, PHOTO-CONVERSION-GUIDE, PRICING-3D-CRYSTAL, PRICING-MODEL,
PRODUCT-PAGE-SCAFFOLDING and TAX-DISCLAIMER. Checksums are in the archive manifest.

These are historical business references, NOT approved current inventory or automatic storefront copy.
Vendor instructions, estimated costs, personal/internal information and superseded platform references must not be exposed to customers.

## Coverage required

- Drinkware: MakerTwist tumblers, electroplated tumblers, water bottles.
- Glassware/bar: Libbey mixing/DOF glasses, square shots, boxed sets, decanters, smoker kits, openers, wine stoppers.
- Kitchen: acacia/bamboo boards and board sets, acacia salt rimmers.
- Small gifts: journals, wood keychains, RFID money clips and accessories.
- Jewelry/pets: balsa earrings, stainless pendants, pet tags/bowls, plus relevant crystal keepsakes.
- Surface ornaments: distinguish surface engraving from subsurface photo products.
- 3D crystals: keep-list shapes/sizes, photographs, inscriptions and compatible bases.
- Custom/bulk jobs and live events: separate quotation and capacity/booking workflows.
- Future studio merchandise: reserved for later; not an active launch claim.

## Decisions needing owner confirmation before selling

1. Photo preparation: FAQ/product copy describes free optimization; crystal pricing lists a paid background/optimization add-on.
2. Turnaround: 24-hour post-approval shipping, longer example email timelines, and color-fill curing requirements must be reconciled.
3. Shared-design bulk rates must not apply to distinct individual names without the approved rule.
4. Current inventory, landed costs, shipping weights, rates, tax treatment and geographic service area need verification.
5. Some items mentioned in FAQ (jewelry/pets) lack full product specifications and approved photography.
6. Discount eligibility, stacking, per-event codes and deposits require owner-approved current policies.
7. Product copy contains possible spelling, machine/process and image-brightness inconsistencies; preserve sources but review public copy.
8. Tax/legal/privacy/refund content needs current qualified review; old estimates are not validated here.
9. The approved logo/photographs may exist outside this repo. Do not manufacture an approved asset or a real-product photograph.

`packages/contracts/catalog.reference.json` is a sanitized navigation/reference fixture only:
no prices, purchasable inventory, delivery commitments, supplier costs or personal records are seeded.
