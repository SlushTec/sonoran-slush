## Engraving notes (production)
- Primary face: top ring of the rimmer (most visible when in use)
- Optional: top of lid
- Keep artwork simple and high-contrast; fine serifs can fill with salt dust
- Avoid deep fills that trap moisture in the wood grain
- Test pass on first of a new design before full run
