## Finish options

| Finish | Description | Lead time impact |
|--------|-------------|------------------|
| **Engrave only** (frosted etch) | Standard laser mark | Standard SLA |
| **Color fill** | Enamel paint wiped into engraving, then **baked/cured** | **+ lead time** (see color-fill policy) |

Color fill is optional and **not same-day**. Cure/bake time is real shop time — set customer expectations on the product page.
