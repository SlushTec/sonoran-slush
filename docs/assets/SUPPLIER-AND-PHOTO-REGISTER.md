## Crystal limits and source precedence

The owner-confirmed keep-list in `docs/CRYSTAL-SIZE-KEEP-LIST.md` controls the selectable sizes. The current supplier site may advertise larger or new shapes; those do not expand this catalog. Small notched and round photo-ornament dimensions remain subject to confirmation. The 2D photo necklaces and 2D round photo ornament are labeled separately from 3D subsurface pieces. Surface-engraved ornaments remain a different product family.

The original crystal-collection bookmark opens a shape chooser. Existing saved designs with a recognized legacy shape migrate to that shape's listing without changing inscription, quantity, supported size, gift details, or record identity. The local request API enforces the selected product's shape and size and rejects unsupported sizes, mismatched shapes, and invalid dog-bone orientations.

`product-sources.json` records each source page, original image URL, source-byte SHA-256, optimized asset SHA-256, local path, captions, and match status. `apps/storefront/src/lib/product-media.json` contains only the public display metadata. No wholesale cost, supplier stock count, delivery promise, customer artwork, or supplier marketing claim is imported as studio business data.
