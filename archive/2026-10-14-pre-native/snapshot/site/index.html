Why crystal

A photograph is a memory. Crystal is how you keep it.

Subsurface laser engraving places thousands of tiny points of light inside optical crystal. Dark areas in your photo become clearer, brighter points. The result is a three-dimensional image you can turn in your hand — a keepsake that doesn’t dim when the screen locks.
