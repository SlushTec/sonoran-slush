## Customer-facing disclaimer (use on site, invoices, event menus)

> **Prices shown do not include tax.** Applicable sales tax (Arizona transaction privilege tax and local taxes) is calculated at checkout or added on the event invoice based on the ship-to or event location. Tax rates vary by city and jurisdiction.

**Shorter footer version:**  
> Prices exclude tax. Tax calculated at checkout / on your event invoice.

---

## How we handle tax

| Channel | Practice |
|---------|----------|
| **Online (Shopify)** | Product prices are **pre-tax**. Shopify Tax (or manual rates) calculates AZ TPT + local on the taxable amount after discounts. |
| **Events / on-site** | Package and menu prices are **pre-tax**. Tax applied on the final invoice for the event location (or as required by AZ rules). |
| **Discounts** | Tax is computed on the **discounted** taxable subtotal. |
| **Shipping** | Tax on shipping follows current AZ / Shopify tax settings for your nexus. |

Tucson-area combined rates are typically around **~8.7%** but **exact rate depends on location** — do not hard-code a single % on product pages as the final tax.
