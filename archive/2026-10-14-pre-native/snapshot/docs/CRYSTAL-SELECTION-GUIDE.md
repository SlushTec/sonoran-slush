# Crystal Selection Guide

**For Sonoran Slush Creative Creations — Jet Mini Original**

Only shapes and sizes the Jet Mini Original can produce (max ≈ 6″ × 4″ / 15 × 10 cm) are listed here.

---

## How to Choose

1. **Start with the photo** — portrait, landscape, group, pet, or single subject?
2. **Decide the use** — desk display, keychain, award, guest favor, memorial, ornament, or gift?
3. **Pick size** — smaller = faster & more portable; larger = more presence and detail.
4. **Add a base or light** if the piece will be displayed (strongly recommended for most desk/gift crystals).
5. **Optional inscription** — short text, date, or names.

---

## Recommended Crystal Styles (Jet Mini Original)

### Rectangle Tall
- Best for: single person, couple portraits, graduation, formal headshots
- Sizes: Small → Mini Mantel (up to 6″ × 4″)
- Strong desk presence

### Rectangle Wide
- Best for: group photos, family shots, scenic moments, pets side-by-side
- Sizes: Small → Mini Mantel (up to 6″ × 4″)

### Heart
- Best for: couples, anniversaries, weddings, romantic gifts, memorials
- Sizes: Small / Medium / Large (up to 5″)
- High emotional weight

### Prestige Iceberg (Small)
- Best for: awards, corporate gifts, milestone recognition, premium keepsakes
- Size: Small only (≈ 5″)
- Works well with bases and lighting

### Cut Corner Diamond
- Best for: gifts, awards, modern look
- Sizes: Small / Medium / Large (up to 3″ cube)

### 3D Dog Bone
- Best for: pet memorials, pet gifts, pet-themed events
- One size: 6″ × 4″ (vertical or horizontal)

### Candle
- Best for: memorials, quiet gifts, table accents
- One size: 4″ × 2.4 × 2.4

### Small Notched
- Best for: compact desk gifts, awards, modern keepsakes
- Confirmed producible on Jet Mini Original

### Circle Ornament
- Best for: holidays, memorials, tree ornaments, year-round keepsakes
- Confirmed producible on Jet Mini Original

### Keychains
- Heart Keychain and Rectangle Keychain
- Best for: guest favors, on-site event giveaways, everyday carry
- Fastest burn times — ideal for live production

### Necklaces (2D Crystal)
- Heart Necklace and Rectangle Necklace
- Best for: wearable keepsakes, smaller gifts

## Size Guidance Summary

| Size band | Approx. range | Best for |
|-----------|---------------|----------|
| Keychain / Jewelry | ~1–3.5 cm class | Favors, everyday carry, on-site volume |
| Small–Medium | ~2.5–4″ | Most personal gifts, desk crystals |
| Large / Mini Mantel | up to **6″ × 4″** | Statement pieces, awards, max detail within capacity |

Exact product dimensions will be listed on the Shopify catalog. Never promise sizes beyond Mini Mantel / 6×4.

## Photo Tips for Best Results

- Clear subject, good contrast, simple background when possible
- We digitize, enhance, and optimize every photo at no extra charge
- Groups: closer crops usually work better than very wide distant shots

## Occasion Quick Picks

- **Wedding / Anniversary** → Heart or tall rectangle + light base + inscription
- **Graduation** → Tall rectangle or prestige small + “Class of …”
- **Memorial / Celebration of Life** → Rectangle, heart, candle, or circle ornament
- **Corporate / Award** → Prestige small or larger rectangle + wooden base
- **Guest favor / Event** → Keychain or small ornament
- **Pet** → Dog bone or medium rectangle/heart
- **Holiday** → Circle ornament
