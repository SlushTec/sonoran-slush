# Sonoran Slush Creative Creations — Company Overview

## Identity

- **Legal / Operating Name:** Sonoran Slush Creative Creations
- **Parent / Umbrella:** SlushTec
- **Location:** Tucson, Arizona
- **Domain:** sonoranslush.net
- **Type:** Fully custom-order laser engraving and personalized gift business + portable on-site event service

## Core Offering

Primary product line centers on **3D subsurface laser-engraved crystal gifts** created from customer-supplied photographs. Supporting product lines include custom barware, salt rimmers, cutting boards, jewelry, and pet items.

**Major differentiator:** First company in Arizona with a portable professional-quality 3D laser engraver capable of on-site production at social events.

## Ordering Model

1. **Online / ship-to-customer** — photo upload → product options → preview → approve → 24-hour production & ship (major holidays excepted).
2. **On-site event service** — we travel to the gathering (weddings, graduations, reunions, corporate, memorials, festivals, and more) and produce finished keepsakes live for guests.

Service area emphasis: Arizona, New Mexico, and Southern California.

## Strategic Role Inside SlushTec

1. Near-term revenue and local brand presence via custom gifts and on-site events.
2. Future official merchandise channel for SlushTec intellectual property (VARP, Ashcourt — Hell Rises, studio apparel, residual-themed items, etc.).
3. Keeps manufacturing / fulfillment capability in-house under the same ownership structure.

## Constraints & Philosophy

- Home / small-shop based operation with portable production capability
- Emphasis on quality and personalization over high-volume commodity product
- No external investors with directional control (aligned with overall SlushTec posture)
