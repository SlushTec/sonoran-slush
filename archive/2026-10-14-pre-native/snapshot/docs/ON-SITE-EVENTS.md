# On-Site & Social Event Services

**Core differentiator:** Sonoran Slush is the first company in Arizona with a **portable professional-quality 3D laser engraver** that can operate on location.

This is not a table-top novelty. It is production-grade equipment brought to the event so guests leave with a finished, permanent keepsake the same day.

---

## Positioning

> Your guests don’t wait for shipping.  
> They watch the memory become permanent — then take it home.

The portable 3D crystal / laser capability turns any gathering into a live “memory station.” Guests provide a photo (or we capture one on site), we digitize and optimize it, engrave it into K9 crystal or other materials, and hand them a finished piece before the event ends.

**Service area focus:** Arizona, New Mexico, and Southern California.
