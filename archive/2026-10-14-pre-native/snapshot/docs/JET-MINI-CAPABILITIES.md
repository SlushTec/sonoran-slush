# 3D LaserBox Jet Mini Original — Capabilities

**Equipment behind Sonoran Slush’s portable / on-site 3D crystal service.**

Sonoran Slush operates a **Jet Mini Original** — a compact, air-cooled, green-beam subsurface laser system built for professional 3D crystal engraving that travels to events.

The Original handles the full range of work (small keychains through larger keepsakes and awards). There is no need for a smaller Express unit.

---

## Why It Matters for Sonoran Slush

- Supports the first-in-Arizona portable professional-quality 3D crystal engraver claim.
- Enables live production at weddings, graduations, reunions, corporate events, markets, and other social gatherings across AZ / NM / Southern California.
- Guests can watch the piece being made and leave with a finished keepsake the same day.

## Machine Specs (Jet Mini Original)

| Spec | Value |
|------|--------|
| **Max crystal size** | ~6″ × 4″ (15 × 10 cm) |
| **Field of view (height)** | ~12 cm |
| **Machine weight** | ~49 lb / 23 kg |
| **Approx. dimensions** | 10.25 × 13.25 × 18.5 in |
| **Travel case** | Yes (portable for events) |
| **Burn speed** | ~100,000 points per minute |
| **Laser** | Green-beam (532 nm class) diode-pumped subsurface |
| **Cooling** | Air-cooled (no water chiller) |
| **Power** | Standard household outlets (~500 W class, 110–220 V) |
| **Noise** | Low (event / office friendly) |

**Typical times**
- Keychains / small pieces → 1–2 minutes
- Medium blocks → roughly 5–8 minutes

## Core Technical Capabilities

- **Process:** True internal 3D point-cloud engraving inside optical / K9 crystal (not surface etching or UV novelty marks)
- **Workflow:** Designed for AI / Cockpit3D-style 2D→3D conversion so a customer photo becomes a producible point cloud quickly
- **Visual engagement:** Transparent / filtered door lets guests watch the engraving happen live
- **Range:** Covers small accessories through larger award- and keepsake-sized crystals in one machine

---

## Practical On-Site Strengths

1. **Portable** — Packs into a travel case; movable by one person; sets up on a standard table in minutes.
2. **Fast enough for events** — Small pieces in minutes; medium crystals in single-digit minutes.
3. **Professional quality** — Subsurface 3D (floating image inside the crystal).
4. **Crowd-friendly** — Guests can see the process; production becomes part of the experience.
5. **Standard power** — No special venue electrical requirements in most cases.
6. **Full product variety** — One machine covers the entire crystal size range Sonoran Slush offers.

---

## Typical Workflow (Event or Studio)

1. Customer provides or we capture a photo.
2. Photo is digitized, enhanced, and converted to a 3D point cloud.
3. Customer previews the result in the chosen crystal shape/size.
4. On approval, the Jet Mini Original engraves the piece.
5. Finished crystal is handed over (or shipped within the stated turnaround for remote orders).
