# Future Brand Merchandise Channel

Sonoran Slush Creative Creations will serve as an official sales avenue for SlushTec studio merchandise when those lines are ready.

## Potential Categories (future)

- VARP / residual-themed apparel and accessories
- Ashcourt — Hell Rises related items
- Studio logo / “You act. The world keeps score.” merchandise
- Limited laser-engraved or crystal pieces tied to world packs or campaign milestones
- Small-batch physical artifacts that reinforce permanent residual / living world themes

## Principles

- Merchandise remains under SlushTec creative control
- Quality and brand coherence over volume
- Can leverage existing laser engraving capability for unique physical items
- Sold through the same Shopify storefront (separate collections) or dedicated merch section

## Timing

No fixed launch date. Channel will be activated when studio IP and brand systems are ready for public merchandise.
