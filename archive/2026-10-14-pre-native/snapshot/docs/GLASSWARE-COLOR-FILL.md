# Glassware Color Fill — Policy & Options

**Last updated:** 2026-08-11  
**Applies to:** Libbey 1639, Libbey 2339, 2.2 oz square shots, and future surface-engraved glass SKUs

---

## What it is

After laser engraving, **enamel paint** is worked into the etched recess, excess wiped, then the piece is **baked and cured** so the color locks into the mark.

Result: higher contrast (especially on clear glass) and a more “finished gift” look than frost-only etch.

---

## Production reality (set expectations)

| Step | Notes |
|------|--------|
| Engrave | Same as standard glass job |
| Fill | Hand process — time scales with area and piece count |
| **Bake / cure** | **Not instant** — oven cycle + cool-down; do not rush |
| QC | Check coverage, pinholes, adhesion |

**Customer-facing rule:** Color fill **adds production days**. Never promise same-day color fill.

Suggested SLA copy:
> **Engrave only:** [your standard glass days]  
> **Color fill:** standard + **[X] business days** for bake and cure

*(Fill in X from your real oven schedule — often +2 to +5 business days depending on batching.)*

---

## Configurator options

| Field | Values |
|-------|--------|
| Finish | Engrave only · **Color fill** |
| Fill color | Limited palette (e.g. white, black, gold, silver, red, navy — lock a short list) |
| Placement | Unchanged (1 side / 2 sides / bottom) |

**Pricing:** Color fill is a **paid add-on** (not free). Example uplift used in product docs: roughly **+$4 to +$6 per surface** on shots; scale up slightly on larger Libbey faces if fill area is big.

Suggested add-on (starting):

| Glass type | Color fill add-on (per order line / per glass) |
|------------|-----------------------------------------------|
| 2.2 oz shot | **+$4** (1 face) · **+$6** (2 faces) · extra if bottom filled |
| Libbey 1639 / 2339 | **+$6** (1 face) · **+$10** (2 faces) · bottom +$4 |

Refine after you time a real batch.

---

## Care & claims

- Prefer **hand wash** for color-filled pieces  
- Dishwasher: may shorten fill life depending on detergent/heat  
- Do not market color fill as “permanent like the etch itself” without qualification — the etch is permanent; fill is durable when cured but not indestructible
