Before you order

What converts well inside crystal

Subsurface engraving turns your photo into points of light. Contrast is everything.

Works well

High contrast. Clear faces. Dark clothing or dark backgrounds. Sharp focus. Medium/close framing. One clear subject or a tight group.

Struggles

Flat low-contrast phone shots. Heavy filters. Busy backgrounds. Tiny subjects far away. Screenshots and heavily compressed downloads.

The rule

Black / dark areas → fewer dots → clearer crystal. Light areas pack more points and read brighter. Depth in the shadows helps faces feel alive inside the block.

Background optimize and contrast work help — they don’t invent detail that isn’t in the file. Send the photo that still makes you stop scrolling; we’ll tell you if the crystal will do it justice.
